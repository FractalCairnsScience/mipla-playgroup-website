# Mura Moegi Kaziw Sagulau Playgroup — Design Directions

## Three possible approaches

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tidal Storylines** | A calm, contemporary Torres Strait learning space shaped by the movement of sea currents, mats, and collective storytelling. Warmly practical for facilitators while visibly anchored in place. | 0.047 |
| **Island Field Notes** | An editorial, paper-and-ink facilitator companion using field-note annotations, practical tables, and archival texture. Grounded and print-forward. | 0.081 |
| **Sunlit Gathering** | A light, celebratory community hub led by daylight, pandanus-like weaving patterns, and big inviting activity imagery. Open and family-facing. | 0.029 |

## Chosen direction: Tidal Storylines

### Design Movement

**Contemporary Indigenous-informed editorial design**: a respectful, non-literal interpretation of the Torres Strait’s sea, gathering practices, and oral traditions. It avoids generic pan-Indigenous motifs and presents cultural content with the restraint of a well-made community resource.

### Core Principles

1. **Place before polish.** Sea, island, mat, and community references make the interface feel situated rather than imported.
2. **Practical tools deserve dignity.** Planners, checklists, downloads, and reporting fields are treated as central objects, not utility afterthoughts.
3. **Warm clarity.** Strong hierarchy, generously sized controls, and plain-language labels support use in live sessions and outreach settings.
4. **Living learning.** Visual rhythm and small interactions suggest a changing tide: gentle, legible, never distracting.

### Color Philosophy

The foundation is **reef-water teal** and deep ink blue: clear enough for action, cool enough to reduce cognitive load. A sun-warmed sand neutral gives pages a printable, facilitator-guide quality, while a restrained **coral ochre** marks important actions, progress, and community details. This is a coastal vocabulary, not a rainbow of decoration.

### Layout Paradigm

The app is arranged as a **facilitator’s tide chart**: a fixed left navigation rail for orientation, a flowing content canvas for learning modules, and a right-edge context strip where a user’s session plan and downloads stay close at hand. Sections change density like island shores—an expansive introduction, tighter action shelves, and document-style planning surfaces.

### Signature Elements

1. **Tidal contour lines:** delicate layered curves that frame headings, panels, and section transitions.
2. **Woven planning bands:** horizontal coloured dividers and material swatches that signal activity categories without mimicking sacred artwork.
3. **Round tide markers:** small numbered or status discs used for steps, session timing, checklist progress, and evaluation prompts.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interaction is purposeful and calm. Actions keep clear labels, controls have generous touch targets, and planning selections become visible in a persistent session summary. Feedback is immediate and plain-spoken; downloading a planning record feels like filing a finished field note.

### Animation

Subtle content entries use a 180–240ms opacity-and-translate reveal with the custom ease `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`. Tidal contour accents may drift only on initial page load and only when reduced motion is not requested. Buttons compress slightly on activation, and panel expansions use quick height transitions with no looping or flashy effects.

### Typography System

**DM Serif Display** provides the quiet authority of a printed guide for page titles and key section questions. **Atkinson Hyperlegible** carries all navigation, control labels, descriptions, and planning fields for accessibility at small sizes. Titles are sentence case; action labels are concise, high-contrast, and never all-caps except small category tags.

### Brand Essence

**A Torres Strait playgroup facilitator companion that joins culturally responsive activity ideas, practical planning, and local reporting in one offline-friendly place.**

Personality: **grounded, generous, capable**.

### Brand Voice

The voice is welcoming and direct, treating facilitators as skilled practitioners. Headlines make a promise of use, while calls to action state exactly what happens next.

> “Shape a session that starts with your community.”

> “Save this plan for your outreach record.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a bold circular tide-and-mat symbol: three widening curved bands meeting a central dot, suggesting a gathering place and outward connections. The wordmark, when used, is an intentionally tracked serif-and-sans pairing—not a default font treatment. The standalone symbol is used in the header and favicon.

### Signature Brand Color

**Reef Current — `#057C83`**: a distinct deep teal for principal actions, navigation, links, and the core logo mark.

## Style Decisions

This project will use only respectful, abstract coastal references until Mura supplies approved cultural artwork, logo material, or community photography. User-provided or approved visuals will take precedence over generated decorative imagery.

Every page uses the facilitator tide-chart shell: a left orientation rail, a central working canvas, and a right-edge strip for the planner, print-ready tools and QR access.

Tidal contour lines, woven planning bands and round tide markers are structural components. They appear in the masthead, navigation, tabs, section headings, activity categories, planning status and downloaded material cues.

The circular tide-and-mat mark and serif-and-sans wordmark remain visible above content as well as in the primary navigation. Reef Current (`#057C83`) is reserved for principal navigation and actions.
