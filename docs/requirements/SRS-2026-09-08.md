# mipla playgroup website Software Requirements Specification

Version 0.1 | 8 September 2026 | Draft for product and data owner review

## Purpose and review status

Provide Mura playgroup workers with culturally reviewed activity resources, session planning and locally generated downloadable guides.

Draft 0.1 for owner review; source inspection only, no runtime or deployment assurance. “Implemented requirement” below means behaviour is represented in inspected source code; it does not mean the application or controls passed runtime tests. Documented requirements retain source-stated intent. Proposed acceptance conditions make this draft testable and require owner agreement; they are not evidence of existing conformance.

Governance and ownership plans are proposals, not completed transfers or approvals. The phrase Infoxchange SRS names an external product, not this software requirements specification. The supplied 2011 external guide is historical workflow context and not a current integration contract.

## Scope and people

Primary users: Outreach facilitator; Mura content and cultural approver; Mura data-governance lead; Mura-appointed maintainer and two administrators.

Data sensitivity: Public approved cultural resources; local session/evaluation text can become sensitive; optional worker analytics are future scope only.

Primary workflow: Browse resources, filter activities, create a staged plan, save locally, download PDFs, report necessary information separately under Mura procedures.

Interfaces: Browser localStorage; Client-side jsPDF and QR generation; Static assets and Express serving; Manual external Infoxchange SRS workflow only.

### Excluded scope

- Central collection of child/carer information
- Unapproved worker tracking
- Assumed Infoxchange API connection
- Transfer of Mura ownership by this document

## Requirements and acceptance

Priority uses Must for release gates and Should for desirable improvements. Categories and stable IDs support the cross-project traceability matrix. Source paths below are relative to this repository. A source-derived target states what must be preserved or corrected; acceptance is to be verified in the implementation project.

### Purpose outcomes and exclusions

**MIP-001 Support facilitated session planning**

The product shall support the Learn, Toolkit, Activity library and Session planner workflows for Mura facilitators.

Acceptance: Facilitator can move from an activity guide to a staged local session plan without creating an online client account.

Priority: Must. Evidence: source-code. Maturity: implemented requirement. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: client/src/pages/Home.tsx navItems and pageIds.


### Users roles and authority

**MIP-002 Respect community decision authority**

Mura shall approve content, cultural resources, data collection and publication through nominated responsible roles.

Acceptance: Approval register identifies nominated primary and backup decision makers; no document is mistaken for their approval.

Priority: Must. Evidence: documented. Maturity: constraint. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: MURA_CODE_OWNERSHIP_AND_MAINTENANCE_PLAN.md proposed ownership; handover plan.


### Functional capabilities

**MIP-003 Find activity resources**

Facilitators shall filter activities by text and category and inspect age guidance, duration, materials and instructions.

Acceptance: Known fixture searches return correct cultural, organised, nature, parallel and digital activities; empty search has a useful state.

Priority: Must. Evidence: source-code. Maturity: implemented requirement. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: Home.tsx Activity, activities and activityQuery.

**MIP-004 Create local session plans**

The planner shall create, edit and save named plans with community, session time, activity stages and evaluation fields.

Acceptance: An empty name is rejected; saved stage order and duration match selected activities after reload.

Priority: Must. Evidence: source-code. Maturity: implemented requirement. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: Home.tsx SessionPlan, createEmptyPlan, savePlan.

**MIP-005 Export locally generated resources**

The application shall generate session-plan, checklist and guide PDFs locally on the worker device.

Acceptance: Network inspection detects no plan-content upload during generation; output preserves multi-page text, activity order and labels.

Priority: Must. Evidence: source-code. Maturity: implemented requirement. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: Home.tsx downloadPlanPdf, downloadChecklistPdf and downloadGuidePdf.


### Workflows and exceptions

**MIP-006 Control local saved records**

Saved plans and checklist state shall remain in local browser storage with clear deletion and shared-device guidance.

Acceptance: Reload restores only the current browser's data; user-facing guidance explains device visibility and how to remove temporary records.

Priority: Must. Evidence: documented. Maturity: constraint. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: Home.tsx STORAGE_KEY and CHECKLIST_KEY; docs/INFOXCHANGE_SRS_REPORTING_INSTRUCTIONS.md section 1.

**MIP-007 Approve resource changes**

Content and functional changes shall pass Mura review, preview testing and publication approval through a recorded maintenance workflow.

Acceptance: A changed cultural resource cannot be treated as approved without a source and approval record.

Priority: Must. Evidence: documented. Maturity: constraint. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: MURA_CODE_OWNERSHIP_AND_MAINTENANCE_PLAN.md Maintenance workflow.


### Data and provenance

**MIP-008 Minimise plan data**

The planner shall prohibit client identifiers and explain that attendance/evaluation entries are aggregate session notes; free text shall be reviewed for unnecessary sensitive content.

Acceptance: Field instructions define counts rather than names for children/carers; a sample plan contains no client identifiers and approved notes only.

Priority: Must. Evidence: proposed. Maturity: planned requirement. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: docs/INFOXCHANGE_SRS_REPORTING_INSTRUCTIONS.md section 1; proposed field clarification.

**MIP-009 Govern local retention**

Mura shall approve how long local plans and downloaded PDFs remain on devices and where approved records are retained.

Acceptance: The owner records retention and device rules; deleting a temporary plan follows those rules without assuming browser storage is centrally managed.

Priority: Must. Evidence: documented. Maturity: constraint. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: docs/PLAYGROUP_WEBSITE_HANDOVER_PLAN.md security controls.


### Interfaces and dependencies

**MIP-010 Keep external reporting separate**

Infoxchange reporting shall remain a separate authorised manual workflow until a current approved integration contract is supplied.

Acceptance: Website does not claim to submit reports or collect external credentials; historical instructions warn that current screens and permissions may differ.

Priority: Must. Evidence: documented. Maturity: constraint. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: docs/INFOXCHANGE_SRS_REPORTING_INSTRUCTIONS.md introduction and sections 1 to 6.

**MIP-011 Verify resource availability**

Every downloadable or linked asset shall have a valid deployed path and an approved source and reuse entry.

Acceptance: Automated link check and download walkthrough succeed in release preview; broken assets block affected feature sign-off.

Priority: Must. Evidence: proposed. Maturity: planned requirement. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: Proposed from Home.tsx /manus-storage assets and ownership asset-register requirement.


### Analytics and model behaviour

**MIP-012 Keep usage metrics optional**

Download counts and staff usage dashboards shall remain proposed future features until Mura approves purpose, fields, access and retention.

Acceptance: Current release cannot be described as collecting usage counts; analytics adoption has a separate approved design.

Priority: Must. Evidence: documented. Maturity: constraint. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: docs/PLAYGROUP_EVALUATION_METRICS_REPORT.md status and sections 1 to 6.

**MIP-013 Protect interpretation of workforce metrics**

Any later named-worker resolution shall use restricted Mura-held mapping and approved support purposes; public reports shall be aggregated.

Acceptance: A proposed analytics fixture cannot resolve a worker through public or ordinary reporting access.

Priority: Must. Evidence: documented. Maturity: constraint. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: docs/PLAYGROUP_EVALUATION_METRICS_REPORT.md section 3.


### Quality and accessibility

**MIP-014 Provide accessible planning**

Resource discovery, activity selection, checklist and planner shall work with keyboard navigation and readable mobile layouts.

Acceptance: Facilitator completes a plan at mobile and desktop widths without pointer-only actions; PDF contents remain legible at normal scale.

Priority: Must. Evidence: proposed. Maturity: planned requirement. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: Proposed usability completion.

**MIP-015 Handle browser storage failure**

The planner shall show non-destructive recovery guidance when storage is unavailable, full or contains invalid JSON.

Acceptance: Injected quota and parse errors do not discard unsaved plan text silently; the user can download a copy.

Priority: Must. Evidence: proposed. Maturity: planned requirement. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: Proposed from Home.tsx localStorage reads and writes.


### Security safety and governance

**MIP-016 Retain cultural governance boundaries**

Cultural stories, language, photographs and media shall not be reused across the portfolio without the relevant Mura review and authority.

Acceptance: Each reused asset has specific approved scope; a shared UI module carries no cultural content by default.

Priority: Must. Evidence: documented. Maturity: constraint. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: MURA_CODE_OWNERSHIP_AND_MAINTENANCE_PLAN.md Handover and Maintenance workflow.

**MIP-017 Avoid unapproved tracking**

The initial site shall collect no central child/carer data or individual worker download tracking.

Acceptance: Release network review records outgoing requests and verifies that planner text and personal use events are not transmitted.

Priority: Must. Evidence: documented. Maturity: constraint. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: docs/PLAYGROUP_EVALUATION_METRICS_REPORT.md status and implementation sequence.


### Deployment and operations

**MIP-018 Provide owner-controlled handover**

Handover shall supply source, release history, asset register and configuration under Mura-controlled accounts with two administrators and least privilege.

Acceptance: Two nominated Mura administrators demonstrate independent account access and restoration of a tagged release; no ownership transfer is assumed.

Priority: Must. Evidence: documented. Maturity: constraint. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: MURA_CODE_OWNERSHIP_AND_MAINTENANCE_PLAN.md Handover checklist.

**MIP-019 Maintain approved releases**

The maintainer shall retain a tagged release and restore archive and review access, configuration and assets at least annually and after material owner/provider changes.

Acceptance: Review records and a restore rehearsal identify the current approved site, assets and responsible contacts.

Priority: Must. Evidence: documented. Maturity: constraint. Dependency: Reviewed source snapshot. Source: MURA_CODE_OWNERSHIP_AND_MAINTENANCE_PLAN.md Access backups and continuity.

## Decisions and release gate

1. Mura must confirm cultural/content approvers, two administrators, account/domain/hosting owners and maintainer.
2. Approve exact permitted evaluation fields and local retention; existing free text can admit identifiers.
3. Decide whether any aggregate usage analytics are justified; current site does not establish event collection.
4. Confirm all resource assets are available and approved for the intended audience.

Before sign-off, the product owner shall confirm scope and users; the data custodian shall approve source and data-handling rules; the technical owner shall demonstrate the Must acceptance conditions and record unresolved defects. Roles above are accountable role proposals, not named-person appointments. No production safety, legal compliance or approval is asserted by this draft.
